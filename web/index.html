    What this page covers

    Wheelhouse is a free, independent page for people who hold Squeeks. It reads public information straight from the Sepolia test network, so you can look around without connecting a wallet. Nothing is sent from your wallet unless you approve it there.

    • How it works: how merging, levels, cooldowns and SQK rewards work.
    • Can I trust what I see?: how this page double-checks the numbers before showing them.
    • What works right now: which actions are available on this network.
    • Contracts: the official contract addresses this page talks to.
    • Developers: tools for building on Squeekchain.

    How it works

    You can merge two Squeeks of the same level that are held the same way (both in your wallet, or both deposited). One of them is burned for good. The one you keep stays the same Squeek with the same picture, goes up one level, and keeps the unclaimed SQK from both. Both Squeeks must be off cooldown. The highest level is 10.

    Cooldowns are counted in Squeek blocks made by the hamster, not in hours or days.

    A checkpoint turns finished payout rounds into SQK rewards that holders can claim. Anyone can run one; whoever does pays the gas and gets no reward for it. Rewards are claimed through staking, including if you only hold SQK.

    Can I trust what I see?

    This page reads the Sepolia network through your connected wallet, or, without a wallet, straight from your browser to free public network servers. There is no Wheelhouse server in between. Every reading is checked against two independent servers; if they disagree, the page stops instead of guessing.

    Before each action, we check the network, supported contracts, ownership and permissions using two independent connections. Any mismatch stops the action.
